About Aasare

Welcome to Aasare NeuroPsychiatric Centre, your trusted destination for comprehensive mental health care and support. We offer a wide range of mental health services, including providing care that is trauma-informed, queer-friendly, and tailored to women’s mental health. Our dedicated team is committed to create a safe space and empower all individuals on their journey towards mental wellness. Discover the path to lasting change at Aasare NeuroPsychiatric Centre.
